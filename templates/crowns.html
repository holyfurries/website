CITY BUILDER / ROGUELIKE / IN DEVELOPMENT

Crowns Adrift.

A roguelike city builder set in the Late Bronze Age.

See how it plays↓

Grow a palace city from a hand of cards. Recruit an army through the households and military buildings you place, and keep it supplied as drought, raiders, and famine threaten your island.

DECKBUILDING

Build your deck as you build your city.

Your citizens petition for new buildings, households, and decrees. Choose which cards to add to your deck, then play them onto the island.

The city adds cards of its own. Build a Training Yard to gain Drill cards that strengthen your troops. Supply farmer households nearby to make those cards more powerful.

CITY BUILDING

Build around the island.

Each run starts on a generated island. Fields yield more grain on floodplains, fisheries need a coast, and mines need hills or mountains. Fit your production chains around the terrain and keep workplaces within reach of workers.

PRODUCTION

Feed your people. Supply your army.

Fields

Harvest grain to feed households and supply barracks. A staffed, supplied barracks deploys a levy every year.

Granary

Keep nearby surplus grain for next year instead of selling it. Stored grain also strengthens recruitment through a Muster Hall.

Foundry

Turn copper and tin into Bronze Tools cards. Spend them on permanent workplace upgrades or use them to damage an enemy.

AUTO BATTLER

Send your army into battle.

Play troop cards to deploy now or queue reinforcements for a later year. Train and equip your soldiers before ending the year, when they fight enemy waves automatically.

Survivors keep their wounds and equipment between encounters. Reinforce the veterans you have, or recruit fresh troops before the next attack.
